The Pitch — Keep It Short
Opening
"Hey, this is Elise calling from SolarInstallersTX.com — [Company Name] is already listed on our directory. I'm reaching out because we want to give you a free Verified Installer badge for your website and bump your listing to the top of [City]. Takes about 2 minutes to claim. You got a sec?"
If They Say "What Is This?"
"We're a Texas-specific solar directory — we connect homeowners looking for installers in their city with verified local pros. You're already in our database. The badge just lets you claim your profile and show up as verified. It's completely free."
The Close — If They're Interested
"Perfect — what's the best email to send you the badge embed code and your verified listing link? I'll get it out to you right now."
Voicemail Script
"Hey, this is Elise from SolarInstallersTX.com. I'm calling because [Company] is already listed on our directory and I wanted to offer you a free Verified Installer badge for your site — no cost, no commitment. Give me a call back at [your number] or I'll try you again tomorrow. Thanks!"
When They Say Yes — Next Steps
  • 1
    Get their email address. Spell it back to confirm. This is the most important thing.
  • 2
    Ask: "Is [Company Name] the right business name for the listing?" — confirm spelling.
  • 3
    Ask: "And the website you want the badge to link to — is it [website from our list]?"
  • 4
    Tell them: "You'll get an email in the next few minutes with your badge code. It just takes a copy-paste to install."
  • 5
    Log: Company, Name, Phone, Email, City → mark as INTERESTED in the spreadsheet.
  • 6
    If they ask about upgrades: "We'll follow up once your badge is live. There are paid tiers for exclusive leads but zero pressure today."
Objection Handling
"I've never heard of your site."
"We're newer but already ranking for solar installer searches across Texas. You actually came up in our installer database which is why I'm calling — we want to make sure your listing is accurate and give you the badge."
"How much does it cost?"
The badge is completely free. "There's no cost at all. We have a premium featured placement for installers who want leads sent directly to them — that's $99/month — but there's zero pressure today. The badge is just a free way to get your name in front of homeowners searching in [City]."
"I'm not interested."
"No problem at all — your listing stays live either way. I just wanted to make sure you knew about the free verification so your site gets the SEO boost too. Have a great day!"
→ Mark as Not Interested, move on. Don't push.
"Can you send me an email instead?"
"Absolutely. What's the best address?" — Get the email, end the call, follow up immediately. Email with subject: "Your Free Verified Badge — SolarInstallersTX.com"
"Who are you again? Is this a sales call?"
"Totally fair question — I'm not selling anything today. Your company is already listed on our directory for free. I'm just offering to verify the listing and give you a badge for your site. That's it."
"We already use EnergySage / another directory."
"That's great — this doesn't replace anything you're doing. It's a Texas-specific directory so it's more targeted for local homeowners in [City]. And it's free. Most installers we talk to are on 3-4 directories."
