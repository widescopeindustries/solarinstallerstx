## 🎓 TRAINING TIPS FOR SALES TEAM

### DO:
✅ Be friendly and consultative (not pushy)
✅ Listen more than you talk
✅ Use their company name frequently
✅ Speak in terms of THEIR benefits (more leads, more revenue)
✅ Have confidence in the product
✅ Follow up relentlessly but respectfully

### DON'T:
❌ Sound scripted (practice until it's natural)
❌ Argue with objections (acknowledge and redirect)
❌ Give up after one "no"
❌ Over-promise results
❌ Bad-mouth competitors
❌ Rush through the pitch

## 📞 ROLE-PLAY SCENARIOS

**Practice these with your sales team before launching:**

**Scenario 1: Friendly, Open Decision Maker**
- They're interested immediately
- Ask good questions
- Practice smooth transition from badge → partner package

**Scenario 2: Skeptical, Been-Burned-Before**
- They've tried directories before and it didn't work
- They're wary of "another sales pitch"
- Practice empathy + differentiation

**Scenario 3: Budget-Conscious Small Installer**
- They like the idea but hesitant on price
- Practice value framing (cost per lead vs flat rate)
- Offer trial/pilot program

**Scenario 4: Large Installer with Existing Marketing**
- They already have good lead flow
- Practice positioning as "diversification" not replacement
- Emphasize passive income stream

**Scenario 5: Gatekeeper / Not Decision Maker**
- Receptionist or admin answers
- Practice getting transferred or getting callback number
