## 📊 Why You Need a Google Business Profile

As a **directory and comparison platform** for solar installers, a GBP will help you:

1. **Appear in Local Search Results** - Show up when users search "solar installers near me" or "compare solar companies Texas"
2. **Build Trust** - Verified business badge increases credibility
3. **Drive Traffic** - Direct link to solarinstallerstx.com from Google Search/Maps
4. **Collect Reviews** - Build social proof for your platform
5. **Share Updates** - Post content about new installers, guides, promotions
6. **Answer Questions** - Engage with potential users via Q&A
7. **Track Performance** - Access analytics on searches, views, clicks

## 🎯 Recommended Business Category

**Primary Category**: `Website Designer` or `Marketing Agency` or `Referral Service`

**Why NOT "Solar Energy Company"?**
You are a **platform/directory**, not an installer. Using installer categories could:
- Confuse customers looking for actual installation services
- Violate Google's guidelines (misrepresenting business type)
- Compete directly with your listed installers

**Alternative Categories to Consider**:
- Internet Marketing Service
- Business to Business Service
- Consultant
- Information Service

**Secondary Categories** (up to 9 additional):
- Consider: Solar Energy Consultant, Solar Energy Equipment Supplier (if applicable)
