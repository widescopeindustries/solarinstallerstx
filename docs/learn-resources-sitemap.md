## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Solar Questions

**Is solar worth it in Texas?**  
Yes! Texas has abundant sunshine (average 300+ sunny days), high electricity rates, and excellent incentives. Most Texas homeowners see 6-8 year payback periods with 25+ years of energy savings. The 30% federal tax credit combined with property tax exemptions make solar highly cost-effective in Texas.

**How much do solar installers charge in Texas?**  
Solar installation costs in Texas typically range from $15,000 to $25,000 for residential systems (before incentives). After the 30% federal tax credit, net costs range from $10,500 to $17,500. Actual costs vary by system size, panel quality, roof complexity, and location.

**Are there rebates for solar panels in Texas?**  
Yes! Available rebates include:
- 30% Federal Solar Investment Tax Credit
- 100% Property Tax Exemption for solar equipment
- Utility rebates (CPS Energy, Oncor, CenterPoint)
- Net metering credits
- City-specific incentive programs

**How long does solar installation take?**  
Physical installation: 1-3 days  
Complete process (permits to activation): 4-8 weeks  
Factors affecting timeline: permit approval, utility interconnection, inspection scheduling

**Do I need permits for solar in Texas?**  
Yes, all Texas solar installations require building and electrical permits. Your NABCEP certified installer will handle the permit application process. Requirements vary by city and county.

**What is net metering in Texas?**  
Net metering allows you to sell excess solar energy back to the grid for bill credits. Texas has competitive retail electricity providers, so net metering policies vary by utility. Most major utilities offer some form of net metering or buy-back program.
