## Phase 9: Legal & Quality Control

### Important Notes:

1. **No Reciprocal Link Schemes**:
   - Don't explicitly say "we link to you, you link to us"
   - Frame it as "partnership" and "mutual benefit"
   - Google is okay with natural reciprocal links in context

2. **Quality Over Quantity**:
   - Don't push spammy sites to link
   - If they have a low-quality site, skip the follow-up
   - Bad backlinks hurt more than they help

3. **Anchor Text Variety**:
   - Some should use company name
   - Some should use "SolarInstallersTX.com"
   - Some should use "Find us on [domain]"
   - Natural diversity is key

4. **Track Toxic Links**:
   - Use Ahrefs/Moz to monitor new backlinks
   - Disavow any spammy sites that link without permission

        contact_name=row['contact_name'],
        company_name=row['company_name'],
