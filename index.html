Texas Solar Incentives and Financing

Federal Solar Tax Credit

The federal solar tax credit provides a 26% credit on solar installation costs through 2032, then decreases to 22% in 2033. This credit applies to both residential and commercial installations and can be claimed on your federal income taxes.

Texas State Incentives

While Texas doesn't offer state-level solar rebates, many local utilities provide incentives. Austin Energy offers up to $2,500 rebates, CPS Energy provides rebates up to $2,500, and Oncor territory offers net metering programs.

Solar Financing Options

Multiple financing options make solar accessible: solar loans with low interest rates, solar leases with no upfront cost, power purchase agreements (PPAs), and cash purchases for maximum savings. Our certified installers can help you choose the best option.
